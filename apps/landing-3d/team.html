Why we
built genie

Asking for gifts is awkward, and getting the wrong ones is worse. So we built a way to ask once — a real wishlist and one shareable link — and let friends send exactly what you wanted, openly or as a surprise.

Behind every gift there’s a merchant who ships it and a concierge who’ll source anything that isn’t on the shelf yet. That’s the team you’ll meet below.
